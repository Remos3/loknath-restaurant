Our philosophy

Simple ingredients, patient flame.

At Oriel, every plate begins at the hearth. We cook with oak and fruitwood, buy from farms within a hundred miles, and let the season decide the menu.

The result is food that feels honest and quietly generous, served in a room made for lingering.
