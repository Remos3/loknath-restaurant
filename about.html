About us

Lokenath Restaurant & Hotel.

On Main Road in Ramkrishna Nagar, Karimganj, Assam. Rice meals, biriyani, Indo-Chinese favourites, momo, rolls, snacks and more.

What we serve

A menu with a lot to choose from.

  1. 01

    Rice & biriyani

    Biriyani, fried rice and pulao, plus rice meals (থালী) with mutton, chicken, egg or several kinds of fish. There is a vegetarian meal too.

  2. 02

    Indo-Chinese & momo

    Chawmein, Schezwan rice, soups and momo in steam, fried, pan fried and butter toasted styles.

  3. 03

    Snacks & drinks

    Rolls, burgers, pokora, chat, paratha and roti, with coffee, lassi and shakes.
