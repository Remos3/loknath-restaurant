DISH PHOTOS GO IN THIS FOLDER
=============================
One photo per dish, named exactly like the list in dish-photo-list.xlsx
(for example mutton-biriyani.jpg, chicken-roll.jpg).

Full and Half sizes of a dish share one photo.
Make them square, about 400 x 400 px, under 100 KB.
The easy way: open tools/photo-tool.html on your site, pick the photos,
download the ZIP, extract it and upload the photos here.

If a photo is missing, the menu shows a drawn illustration for that dish.
